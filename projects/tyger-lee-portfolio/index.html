Hello

Hi, I’m Tyger Lee.

Design isn’t a side quest for me — it’s how I make sense of everything. I sketch on receipts, rename fonts in my head and redesign menus while I wait for food.

I chase ideas that make people stop scrolling: marks with teeth, type with attitude and illustration that feels hand-made, because it is.

Right now I’m studying graphic design and taking on brands, artists and small businesses who want to look as loud as they feel.
